Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about StudentHub.

01

What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is a centralized student portal that provides access to academic information, events, notices, profiles and student services.

02

How can I register on StudentHub?

Click on the Register option from the navigation menu and enter the required student information to create your account.

03

How can I access my dashboard?

Login to your StudentHub account using your registered credentials. After successful login, you will be directed to your student dashboard.

04

Where can I check my attendance?

Your attendance information is available under the Attendance section of the student dashboard.

05

Where can I find study materials?

Study materials uploaded by faculty can be accessed through the Study Materials section of the dashboard.

06

How can I contact StudentHub support?

You can contact the StudentHub support team through the Contact Us page using the provided email, phone number or contact form.

Still Have Questions?
